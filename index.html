How I Build High-Performing Shorts

Every second counts in vertical content. Here is how I maximize audience retention.

3-Second Hook Craft

Curating high-energy visual intros, questions, and audio stingers to prevent immediate user drop-off.

Kinetic Subtitles

Accurate, animated text syncing designed for the 70%+ of viewers watching silently in public.

Pacing & Rhythmic Cuts

Eliminating dead air, tightening pauses, and matching transition points directly with soundtrack downbeats.

Safe-Zone Framing

Carefully balancing text and graphics away from TikTok / Reel icons, comment trays, and UI buttons.
